How a check works

  1. Gemini watches the video

    It listens to every speaker and reads on-screen text, then lists the factual claims with timestamps. Opinions and jokes are skipped.

  2. Each claim is checked on Google Search

    Official, research, fact-checker and news sources are weighed. Social media doesn't count. No source means no verdict.

  3. You see the evidence

    Tap a claim to jump to that moment in the video and read what the sources say. Share the result with one link.
